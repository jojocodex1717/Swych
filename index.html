How it works

Trade without ever meeting up

The Swych-Box

Less like a locker, more like a watchful vending system

Each Swych-Box is a self-serve drop point where several students can trade at the same time.

  • 16 small compartments (30 × 30 cm) on one side for everyday items, and 2 tall compartments on the other for bigger things.
  • PIN pad and tap/insert payment terminal on the two remaining sides, so buyers and sellers check out on their own side.
  • A fresh PIN every time, like an Uber ride code. The box already knows your item and lights the right door red.
  • Sensors confirm an item was actually placed (or removed) before the door locks, so nobody can fake a listing.
  • Self-closing doors give a "stand clear" warning and stop instantly if a hand or strap is in the way. If a door is stuck for over 5 minutes, our support team is alerted and can check the camera.
  • Pay in the app or at the box. Funds go to the seller automatically.

Our mission

Affordable, accessible essentials for every student

To give university students an online space to buy, rent, trade and swap items, giving them affordable and accessible access to essential resources.

Our vision

To create connected university communities where students can easily share resources, making student life more affordable and accessible.

The problem

Essentials cost too much and take too long to get

  • Students need textbooks, notebooks, calculators, athletic gear, dorm furniture and kitchen appliances.
  • The University of Toronto recommends budgeting $1,000 to $2,000 a year for books and supplies.
  • Everyday items are used briefly, then thrown away, and leftover supplies have nowhere to go.
  • Busy schedules make selling or buying second-hand a hassle.
Our solution

An app and website just for students

Swych lets students sell, rent or swap university essentials in a way that's safe, simple and accessible, with Swych-Box smart lockers on campus for drop-off and pickup.

In one line: a convenient online marketplace where busy university students can buy, sell, rent and swap essential campus items with fellow students.
What makes us different

Safety built in, digitally and physically

🎓 Student-only

Students verify their university email at sign-up and again once a year, so the marketplace stays student-only as people graduate.

🔐 No face-to-face

Swych-Boxes on campus and one-time PINs mean you never have to meet a stranger or rearrange your schedule.

📍 Campus networks

Each university has its own trading network, so everything is close by and the people are fellow students.

Who we serve

Students in transition

  • All university students: bachelor's, master's and PhD
  • Incoming first-years, who may not know many people yet
  • Students moving between stages, like second to third year or bachelor's to master's
  • Early adopters: students strapped for cash, students moving residences and students with busy schedules

Business plan

How Swych makes money

1. 8% transaction fee

Taken from the seller's proceeds at payment, the same way eBay, Poshmark and Mercari do it. It applies to sales and rentals only; swaps are free because no money changes hands. At a $30 average item price, that's $2.40 per transaction.

Instead of claiming to be the cheapest, our pitch is what the box adds that a free listing can't: verified drop-off and pickup, no meeting strangers and built-in payment instead of cash or e-transfer risk.

2. Advertising (target: $1.60 per active user per month)

  • Boosted listings at the top of search or a category
  • Ads on the Swych-Box screen when it's idle
  • Brand ads on the app and website
  • Local businesses and campus groups are prioritized

Monthly sponsorships at first, then CPM pricing as we grow.

Break-even

Profitable in about 13 months

Each active user brings in about $5.20 a month ($3.60 in fees + $1.60 in ads), minus about $1.76 in card processing. That leaves $3.45 per user, so every extra 1,000 active users adds about $3,445 a month in profit.

Key metrics

How we'll measure success

  • Campus penetration: the percentage of students at each campus who have the app. It tells us whether our marketing is working.
  • Sales, rentals and swaps per active user: shows engagement and whether students come back.
  • Seller and trader reviews: keeps the community trustworthy.
