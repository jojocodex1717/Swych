# Swych

**Buy. Rent. Swych.** The university marketplace for things that deserve a second life.

Swych is a student-only marketplace where university students buy, rent and swap essentials (textbooks, calculators, dorm gear) and exchange them through **Swych-Box** smart lockers on campus, with no face-to-face meetups.

This repository holds the website prototype built by **Team Violet**: Josephine Hatton, Dennis Zhao, Kristopher Jiang, Emily Chen, Pranav Sandesh and Rayna Geng.

## What the prototype does
- Sign up with a university email (.ca / .edu)
- Browse listings and filter by Buy, Rent or Swap
- Post listings with smart price suggestions
- Reserve items and pay in the app or at the box (simulated)
- Message sellers and receive one-time pickup PINs
- Request Board for items you can't find
- Interactive Swych-Box demo (16 small + 2 tall compartments, red-lit doors, item sensors, self-closing doors)
- Business plan: costs, 8% fee model, break-even calculator, profit projections and timeline

> Prototype note: accounts, listings, messages and codes are saved in each visitor's own browser, and no real payments are taken.
